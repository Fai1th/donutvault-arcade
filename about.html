Design system

Built to feel readable, rewarding, and fast.

The design uses researched UX principles: strong contrast, limited color roles, clear visual hierarchy, predictable spacing, rounded surfaces, and instant feedback states.

Color roles

Dark backgrounds reduce glare and make reward colors pop. Gold signals value, green signals success, cyan marks interaction, pink adds energy.

Visual hierarchy

Large headings, compact labels, and clear card groups guide attention without making the player hunt for controls.

Shape language

Rounded cards feel approachable, square game grids feel precise, and pill buttons make actions obvious.

Feedback

Wins, losses, cashouts, and status messages update immediately so every click feels responsive.
